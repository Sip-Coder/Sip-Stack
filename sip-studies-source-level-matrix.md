## Who it's for
Newcomers to beverage hospitality: front-of-house staff, café and bar teams, event hosts, hospitality students, beverage-curious career changers, and community volunteers who want a grounded start in water before moving toward coffee, tea, beer, spirits, and wine.

## Expected abilities at Source
By completing Source, a learner can:
- **Name** the main water source types and **locate** them on a simple map of landscapes.
- **Recognize** basic sensory cues in still and sparkling water (look, mouthfeel, mineral impression, finish).
- **Describe** how water moves from source to glass, including common treatment steps.
- **Read** the headline lines of a water-quality report or bottle label.
- **Serve** water correctly (temperature, glassware, pour) and speak one clear sentence to a guest.
- **State** one fact about water access for a community connected to the water they serve.

## Roles it prepares for
Water steward (restaurant/hotel) · café or tea-bar lead · event beverage host · front-of-house trainee · hospitality student · Somm Support volunteer · entry beverage-content creator.

## How enrollment gives back
Every Source enrollment sends a set contribution to **Somm Support** water-accessibility projects (clean-water access, disaster-relief water, kids' right to water).

## How to read blank cells
- A **filled cell** = taught and assessed at that level.
- A **blank cell** (shade Mauve-Grey `#817985` at ~15% in print) = *not yet* — not essential at that level. It is intentionally empty, not missing.
- Source cells use **recognition verbs**: name, locate, recognize, describe.
- Flow cells use **connective verbs**: connect, distinguish, recommend, calculate, explain impact. Flow builds on Source.
- **Give-Back** is never blank: every topic ties to Somm Support.
