### How to read the evidence

- **Registration.** Skeleton, muscles and skin in the raised-leg plate come from the same fixed-stepped pose with one camera per hero. The shoulders and hips use displaced articular centers inside the actors. The upper arms follow the posed elbows, and the lower limbs follow the actual fitted knee and ankle. Measurements use actual posed triangles wherever the question is about physical clearance. [Raised-leg registration](raised-leg-registration.md)
- **Coverage.** The body test compares barycentric depth through the live skin and covered skin from six directions at 448 × 448 in each pose. Clothing and hair cannot conceal an exposed bone in this check. An excess of 0.020 in is scored, along with silhouette samples outside the skin. A viewing ray can span an entire limb or an air gap. A separate closest-triangle test measures the physical distance to the nearby skin. [Pose measurements](body-fit.json)
- **Eye and hair fit.** The Axon skull, cornea, lids and hair are tested at their actual coordinates. The hair root correction trims buried cap geometry. Nine views per hero/tier retain the original silhouettes and visible part IDs. In the native shaded comparison, 18 of 5,477,920 RGB pixel positions differ. [Head fit and Hummin signatures](head-fit.json)
- **Performance.** Cold browser visits start at Heros or Village. A separate timer starts when the inspector first scrolls into view. Inspection geometry is generated only for the selected actor. [Method, measurements and source hashes](performance.md)

### Reference decisions

- **HAELE 3D.** The staggered leg raises expose hip rotation and knee alignment on three adjacent bodies. The raised-leg plate holds all three layers at the same instant and reports the joint markers. [Pose study](https://x.com/haele3d/status/2122343603762548994)
- **HanageRuge.** The orbit and exploded parts wall separate structures that overlap from the front. The profile and posterior plates show the scapular spine, sacrum, costal cartilage and individual wrist and ankle bones. [Atlas clip](https://x.com/hanageruge/status/2096404647154163893)
- **omarsar0.** The shoulder close-up shows the pectoral fan turning toward the arm as the overlying layer fades. The inspection muscles use posed origin and insertion points and fit their superficial tissue to the actual skin envelope. The picker film uses four separate structures with live inch readouts. [Shoulder study](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2097353640792985816)
- **Promixyz.** The exploded-assembly and material-layering examples informed the continuous cortical shells and the separation of ivory cortex, cartilage, reddish muscle and pale tendon. The same physical components render assembled and exploded. [Roadmap](https://x.com/Promixyz/status/2123734453486842008)
- **Character identity.** The November 20 sheets establish Sippy’s swept dark hair and Roma’s waves, band and expressive eyes. Their shipped facial surfaces, hairlines and garments supply the outer reference. The face plate shows the eye-to-socket fit and the fidelity of the current exterior. Its measurements describe registration to the live models. Exact likeness to the illustrated sheets has not been established. [Face registration](face-registration.jpg)
- **Construction sources.** OpenStax’s pectoral and pelvic girdles, axial skeleton, hand and foot chapters supply the bone identities and articulations. The proximal carpal row runs scaphoid, lunate, triquetrum and pisiform from the thumb side. The distal row runs trapezium, trapezoid, capitate and hamate. The artwork, videos and research sources supplied visual and dimensional references. All meshes are original procedural constructions.
