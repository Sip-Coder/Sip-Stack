# Facial fat in Axon

The model adds 16 individually named support shapes per hero. The facial fat pads are authored in the donor head frame and share its uniform assembly transform. The nearest-support measurements include these pads. The muscle and skull measurements remain separate. [Pad names and pose](../face-anatomy-proof.json) · [Regional measurements](face-measurements.md)

Axon’s Facial fat row can show, fade or isolate the pads. The row starts off. The face overlay includes the pads as a separately tinted layer with partial opacity to show the registered muscle and skull. [Face and fat study captures](media.md)

The production loader check paired Sippy Mobile with Roma High. Each returned 16 distinct pads and 35 facial muscle meshes in the shared head frame. It also rejected a canceled request, reused cached pads and rejected later requests once the model was disposed.

On the same two actor and tier pairs, Chromium recorded zero facial layer files on the floor and on ordinary Axon open. Isolating the pads fetched one JSON and changed the drawn figure. Opening the face overlay then fetched one muscle GLB and reused the pads. Switching to the face heatmap drew a distinct photograph from the same cache. Hiding or closing the layer restored the exact original Axon pixel digest, with zero console errors. [Browser receipt](face-fat-browser-check.json)
