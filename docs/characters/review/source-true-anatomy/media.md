# Source-true anatomy review

The full-body captures below record the anatomy rebuild. The later facial pass has matched close-ups, expression sheets and its own live film. [Sippy and Roma face studies](faces/media.md)

The outfit plates pair rendered PR #24 characters with the rebuilt Sippy and Roma on the same scale and ground origin. Both rows use the Character Ref renderer and the playable character builders. The capture fixes the orthographic camera at four angles to make shoulder, hip, limb and overall silhouette changes visible.

In the anatomy plates, the source bones retain their BoneHub acquisition pose, including bent elbows. The corresponding Axon row shows each bone held in the character’s neutral pose. A separate pair of rows shows the original BodyParts3D and Z-Anatomy atlas surfaces and their live Axon placements on Sippy. Roma’s plate pairs the male atlas source with the upper-body surfaces adapted to her female skeleton. Her original Andreassen female lower-limb muscles appear in a separate source row at the same camera angles. The original acquisitions come from different people and poses. Gaps and overlaps at the joints remain visible. A uniform scale and translation frame each whole source collection for comparison.

The cameras show the front, anatomical left profile, back and a front view rotated 32° with a 4° elevation. Returning from the last angle to the front reproduced identical native pixels for every study. The capture ledger records the served code and GLB hashes, the front-return checks and checksums for the exported files. [Capture ledger](capture.json)

The film shows the first opening of the actual Heros inspector, an orbit around the bones and muscles, and an axial explode and reassembly with the skin and clothes visible. Roma’s film also isolates her subcutaneous fat and skin. Timing comes from the separately throttled browser runs. [First open, orbit and explode](axon-open-orbit-explode.mp4)

Roma’s matching outer-layer plates include her independent pectoral, gluteal, hip and thigh fat surfaces, the stylized skin shell and the clothed model. The standalone skin shows a seam where the authored torso shell meets the thighs. [Mobile](roma-mobile-outer-layers.jpg) · [High](roma-high-outer-layers.jpg)
